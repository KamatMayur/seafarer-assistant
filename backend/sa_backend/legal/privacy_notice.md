# Privacy notice and tester terms

*Version {version} · Test version of Seafarer Assistant*

Seafarer Assistant is an **independent** app run by **{operator}**. It is **not affiliated with DGMA, DG Shipping
or the Government of India**. Always verify any information with the official circular.

## What we collect and why
| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Your email address | To sign you in (one-time codes) and, if you agree, to email you alerts |
| Profile: name (optional), department, level, rank, home MMD, ship types, exams you are preparing for, language, quiet hours, topics you follow or mute | To send you only the circulars and notices that concern you |
| Certificate details you enter: type, grade/course, number, issuing authority, issue and expiry dates | To remind you before they expire and to tell you when a circular may affect them |
| Alerts sent to you and which ones you have read | To show your feed on all your devices |
| Problem reports you send, and automatic crash reports (error message and app version, no personal data) | To fix bugs |
| Your consents (what you agreed to, and when) | Required by law |

**Certificate scans and photos are never uploaded.** They stay on your device, encrypted.

We do **not** collect your DG Shipping / e-Samudra / e-NAVIK passwords, contacts, location or phone number. We do
not sell or share your data, and there is no advertising.

## Where it is stored and for how long
On a server we rent ({hosting}). Your data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings →
Delete my account) it is removed from the server immediately. Encrypted server backups are kept for up to 14 days, then deleted.

## Your rights (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)
- **See and download** your data: Settings → Download my data.
- **Correct** it: edit your profile and certificates in the app.
- **Delete** it: Settings → Delete my account.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time (deleting the account, or switching off email alerts).
- **Complaints / questions:** write to **{contact}**. We will answer within 7 days. You may also approach the Data
  Protection Board of India.

## Tester terms
- This is a **test version**. It may have bugs, may be unavailable at times, and may change.
- Summaries are **automatic extracts** quoted from the official PDF. They can be incomplete or wrong. **Do not rely
  on the app alone** for anything that matters: always read the official circular and check with DGMA or your MMD.
- No payment is taken. When the test ends you will be told, and test accounts may be deleted.
- You must be 18 or older.
- Please report problems from Settings → Report a problem.
